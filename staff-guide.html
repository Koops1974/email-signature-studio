Adding my email signature

It takes about two minutes. First, pick which email you use, then follow that guide.

Before you start

  1. Open the signature link your school sent you (or the Signature Studio with your template loaded).
  2. Go to the Add your staff tab and enter your name, job title, email and phone — or ask whoever manages it to send you your finished signature.
  3. Click Copy on your signature. (If Copy does nothing, click Download .html, open the file, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C to copy it.)
  4. Now follow the guide below for Outlook web or Gmail.
Outlook web outlook.office.com / school Microsoft 365 Gmail mail.google.com / Google Workspace

⭐ Outlook web

  1. Sign in to outlook.office.com with your school account.
  2. Click the Settings gear ⚙ (top right), then choose Mail and Compose and reply.
  3. Scroll to the Email signature box.
  4. Paste your signature there (Ctrl+V). Give it a name such as “Work”.
  5. Tick “Automatically include my signature on new messages I compose” and, if you like, the “on messages I forward or reply to” option.
  6. Click Save, then open a new email to check it appears correctly.
Logo looks missing or blocked? Outlook hides externally hosted pictures until a receiver allows them. Next to the image, click Download pictures once, then contact the sender for permission if prompted. (This is normal for Outlook and not something wrong with your signature.)

⭐ Gmail

  1. Sign in to mail.google.com with your school Google account.
  2. Click the Settings gear ⚙ (top right), then See all settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Signature section and click Create new.
  4. Name it (for example “Work”) and press Create.
  5. Click inside the signature editor box and paste your signature (Ctrl+V).
  6. Under Signature defaults, select your new signature for New mail (and optionally for On reply/forward).
  7. Click Save Changes at the bottom, then compose a new email to check.
Logo not showing? Gmail normally loads hosted images through its own cache automatically. If yours looks broken, the image link may require a login — tell whoever manages the signatures.

Quick checks

  • Signature not appearing on replies? Check the “reply/forward” setting in Outlook or the “On reply/forward” default in Gmail.
  • Old signature still shows? Please remove the default “sent from my phone” or any duplicate old signature from the same settings box.
  • Something looks broken (missing line, wrong name)? It’s the template — ask the admin who manages signatures to update it on the shared link.
