Privacy

Your data stays in your browser.

How your data is handled

  • No server-side storage. This is a static web page: your template, staff list, CSV files and generated signatures exist only in the browser you use. Nothing is uploaded, logged or stored on a server.
  • Saved to your own browser. “Save” keeps your work in this browser. Export (.json) downloads a backup file and Import restores it, so you can recover if a browser’s saved data is cleared.
  • Share links. A share link includes your template and staff information in the web address so the same setup can be opened on another computer. The information can be seen in the link itself, so only share it with someone you trust.
  • Minimal personal data. Signatures typically contain names and job titles only. If you include email addresses or phone numbers, that is personal data under UK GDPR, so treat your CSV like any other school record and don’t share it externally.
  • Logos. Only the image web address is used here; the image itself is never uploaded. Keep the URL on your school’s own hosting (for example SharePoint) so image requests stay within your control.

Because nothing is stored, there is no database to secure and no separate processor to add to your record of processing.
